    Specimen 01Medusozoa

    Medusa

    A bell of jelly-like mesoglea that rows through the water by contracting. Rings of tentacles carry nematocysts — microscopic stinging capsules that fire on contact.

    Moves by
    Bell pulsing
    Light source
    Bioluminescent proteins
    Specimen 02Teuthida

    Squid

    Eight arms and two longer feeding tentacles tipped with sucker clubs. Many deep-sea squid carry light organs on their undersides to blend into the faint light from above.

    Moves by
    Jet propulsion & fins
    Light source
    Photophores
    Specimen 03Ctenophora

    Comb jelly

    Not a true jellyfish. It swims with eight rows of fused cilia; as they beat they split light into travelling rainbows. Many species also glow blue-green when disturbed.

    Moves by
    Eight beating comb rows
    Light source
    Diffracted light & bioluminescence
    Specimen 04Lophiiformes

    Anglerfish

    A modified dorsal spine forms a rod (illicium) tipped with a lure (esca). The glow comes from bacteria living inside the lure, drawing prey toward a mouth full of needle teeth.

    Moves by
    Slow drift, ambush
    Light source
    Symbiotic bacteria
